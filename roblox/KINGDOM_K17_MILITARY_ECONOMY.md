# K17 Military Economy

K17 projects the economic cost and readiness of a possible military force without creating or commanding one.

Outputs include nominal force size, supply days, supply/equipment readiness, threat and mobilization pressure, projected upkeep, and procurement needs.

K17 does not recruit, form armies, move agents, resolve combat, or consume supplies. Future military/combat authorities may use K17 as an economic input.

Runtime: `Workspace.AstraKingdomState/K17MilitaryEconomy`.
