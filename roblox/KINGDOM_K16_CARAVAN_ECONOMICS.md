# K16 Caravan / Logistics Economics

K16 owns economic caravan plans and outcome accounting only.

It builds cargo value, route-cost/risk, escort cost, expected loss, expected profit and margin. A server-only API supports `CreatePlan`, `RecordOutcome` and `Snapshot`.

K16 never plans paths, moves agents, withdraws Living World resources, transfers inventory, or mutates colony stock. Physical execution belongs to the World/Agent transport authority.

Runtime: `Workspace.AstraKingdomState/K16CaravanEconomics`.
