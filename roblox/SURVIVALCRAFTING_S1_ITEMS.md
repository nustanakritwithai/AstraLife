# SurvivalCrafting S1 — Item & Material Catalog

Canonical item identifiers and metadata for the survival-production track.

Includes raw materials, processed materials, components, food, tools, station kits and building parts. The catalog is data-only and does not mutate Inventory, ResourceEconomy, Construction or Living World resources.

Future S-modules may use the same IDs through composition adapters; S1 itself does not depend on S2-S12.

Acceptance target: `Workspace.AstraSurvivalCraftingState.S1Items.S1Status == "PASS"`.
